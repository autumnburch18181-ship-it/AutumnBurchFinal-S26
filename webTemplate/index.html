Mr. Rat

By Autumn Burch

For my final project I chose to make a short video comic with sound and animated elements. The comic follows a rat, named Mr. Rat, who after many tumultuos human encounters, realizes that humans arent very nice at all.

I decided on this idea because it was the first to come to mind, I ended up really liking it and enjoyed making it. It ties to many political and social causes both directly and indirectly; the message I was aiming to convey with my project is that humans are destructive, not every human, but it almost doesn't matter.

Directed and Animated by Autumn Burch
